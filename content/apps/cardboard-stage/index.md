- Date: 2026-09-25

Cardboard Stage is a free browser tool for composing scene references with your own images. Arrange the scene, frame the shot, and show AI exactly what you mean.

## Your next shot starts here

You can picture the shot. Getting an AI tool to understand the placement, perspective, and framing can be harder. Cardboard Stage gives you a visual way to communicate your idea before you write the prompt.

Build your composition like cardboard cutouts on a tiny film set. Bring in characters, props, and backgrounds, arrange them in the scene, then position the camera until the framing feels right.

**Less guessing. Show AI the shot you mean.**

[Try Cardboard Stage for free](https://cardboardstage.ca/)

## From idea to scene reference

1. **Bring in your images.** Drop in the characters, props, and backgrounds you want to work with.
2. **Build your composition.** Move, scale, and rotate each image. Drag its corners to transform its shape and fit it into the scene.
3. **Find your angle.** Position the camera for a low angle, a view from above, or a closer shot. Explore the framing until it matches your idea.
4. **Capture the scene.** Turn your composition into an image that communicates where everything belongs and how the shot should look.
5. **Take it to your AI tool.** Use the captured image with the AI of your choice. Ask it to create a polished scene reference and write a prompt for your next video.

## Direct the details that matter

- **Placement:** Show where characters and props belong in relation to the background.
- **Scale and shape:** Adjust the size, rotation, and corners of your images to build the composition you have in mind.
- **Camera and framing:** Explore how the scene reads from different angles and distances before capturing it.
- **A visual starting point:** Give your chosen AI tool a concrete reference for the shot you want to develop.

## Built for visual planning

Use Cardboard Stage to block out a video shot, explore a storyboard composition, or explain a scene idea to a collaborator. It is a simple way to turn the picture in your head into a reference you can work from.

Cardboard Stage handles composition and capture. The next step happens in the AI tool you choose, where you can refine the image and develop your video prompt.

## Your scene stays in your browser

Your images and scenes stay in your browser while you work in Cardboard Stage. No project uploads. You choose when to take a captured image to another tool.
